# Guidelines

## Selection Criteria
For each cell type, select 5-15 genes that:
- Are well-established canonical markers for the cell type (e.g., CD markers, transcription factors, lineage-specific genes)
- Have high fold change AND biological relevance — statistical significance alone is not sufficient
- Are specific to the cell type rather than broadly expressed
- Would be recognized by a domain expert as characteristic of this cell type

## What to Avoid
- Ribosomal genes (RPL*, RPS*) unless the cell type is specifically defined by ribosomal activity
- Mitochondrial genes (MT-*) unless biologically relevant to the cell type
- Housekeeping genes that are broadly expressed across cell types
- Genes selected purely for high fold change without biological significance
