## What to Extract

- For group_name: Match group_label to the MOST APPROPRIATE known cell type from the list above

## Normalization Rules
- group_name: Matched known type OR UPPERCASE version of group_label
- feature_name: Convert feature_label to UPPERCASE

## Source Information
- source_type: Always "text" for manuscript text extractions
- source_rationale: Must be a verbatim excerpt from the manuscript that mentions BOTH the cell type and gene
